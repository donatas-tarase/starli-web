How to test your digital product delivery before launch

Written November 3, 2026 · Starli Labs

Illustrative app view.

The product page looks ready and the files have been uploaded. Before announcing your launch, make time to experience the purchase as a customer. A delivery test follows the whole path—from choosing a product to opening the downloaded file—and helps you catch problems while you can still correct them.

This checklist works for a first digital product or for a change to an existing listing. Test with an address and order you control, follow your payment provider’s test instructions, and avoid involving real customers in a trial run.

1. Start at the product page

Read the page as if you have never seen the product before. Can you tell it is a digital download? Does the description explain what is included, the file formats, any software requirements and how access works? If you already sell supported variants, check that their names make the differences clear. Wix currently does not support product options on its native digital products; see the Wix product-options limitation.

Review any download limit, expiry or licence terms you display. The customer-facing promise should match your actual configuration. For example, do not advertise indefinite access if your settings end access after a fixed period.

2. Check the files and product mapping

In Starli Digital Downloads, review the files associated with the product and each variant. Compare them with your listing: the printable option should lead to the printable file, and the extended pack should include the items promised for that option.

Open the source files before testing. Check spelling, working links inside a PDF, image quality and the contents of ZIP archives. Give files clear names that a buyer will recognise. If an application is required to open or edit a file, make sure the requirement is visible on the product page and in the included instructions.

3. Place a controlled test order

Choose a test method appropriate for your Wix store and payment setup. Use a test customer email address you can access, select the exact product or variant, and work through checkout. Record which path you used so you can repeat it after fixing a problem.

Delivery in Starli follows a paid order. Confirm that your test reaches the paid state appropriate for your test method; an unpaid or pending order is not equivalent to a completed delivery test. If you use a delayed payment method, follow the relevant Wix and payment-provider instructions for confirming payment.

Wix’s own digital product delivery has its own flow and emails. If both a native digital product and a separate delivery app are involved, check the customer-facing messages carefully so buyers are not confused by duplicate or different instructions. Wix describes its standard flow in its digital product guide.

4. Open the email like a buyer

Check the recipient, subject and contents. Is it obvious what was purchased and what the customer should do next? Follow the link from the message instead of using an administrative shortcut. Open it on a phone as well as a computer if those devices are relevant to your customers.

Check whether a support contact is easy to find. If you offer customised email design, compare the finished message with your brand and proofread it. Do not rely on a draft preview alone; inspect the message produced by your controlled test order.

5. Download and use the actual files

Download every file offered for the chosen option. Open the files on a suitable device and confirm that they match the product description. For a ZIP file, extract it and check the contents. For templates, try a representative edit. For audio, play the file. If there is an instruction document, follow it from the beginning without using your own prior knowledge.

Keep a short record: product and variant, test date, the files received and any problem found. That record makes it easier to retest a change and distinguish one option from another.

6. Review access and support

Check the order’s access status and activity in the app. If you use download limits or expiry, confirm that the information shown to the buyer agrees with the rules you configured. Use only your own controlled test order to check a limit or support action, and be aware that a download may consume one of the allowed uses.

Think through how you would respond if a buyer cannot find their email or their access has expired. Resending the delivery email does not itself extend expiry, restore stopped access or reset the download count. Review the situation and use the appropriate access controls available on your plan. Our guide to helping a customer access their download walks through the decision.

A quick pre-launch checklist

  • The listing says it is a digital product and explains what is included.
  • Each variant is connected to the correct files.
  • File formats, software requirements and access terms are clear before checkout.
  • A controlled test order reaches the appropriate paid state.
  • The delivery email arrives at the expected address and its link opens.
  • Downloaded files open, and any ZIP contains the intended items.
  • The buyer can find instructions and a way to contact support.
  • You have repeated the test for materially different variants or changes.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to test every product variant?

Test every distinct delivery path you can. At a minimum, check each option that should deliver a different file or bundle; a test of one variant does not establish that all mappings are correct.

Is a preview of the email enough?

A preview is useful for checking layout, but a controlled order also checks the connection between the product, payment state, recipient, link and files. Use both when available.

What if the email arrives but the download does not work?

Review the order, link status, expiry and remaining downloads, then try the correct action for the problem. Keep the test order and customer information under your control while troubleshooting.

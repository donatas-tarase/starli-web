How to set download limits and expiry for Wix digital products

Written November 3, 2026 · Starli Labs

Illustrative app view.

A customer has paid for your digital product. They should be able to get their files easily—and you should understand what happens to their access after the sale. Download limits and expiry dates help you set those expectations before a support question arrives.

Quick answer: A download limit sets how many downloads are allowed, either across an order or per file depending on your settings; an expiry rule sets how long access remains available. Starli Digital Downloads offers custom limits and expiry on eligible plans, alongside tools to review activity and manage customer access. Choose rules that suit your product, and explain them to buyers before checkout.

How digital delivery works in Wix

Wix Stores already delivers digital products when payment is received. Its delivery guide says the standard email download link remains active for 30 days and can be resent. For delayed payments, the download email is sent after the order is marked as paid.

Starli adds a place to connect files to products or variants and manage downloads after a paid order. If you want to choose your own download rules, check which current app plan includes custom limits and expiry. Plan names and features can change.

Download limit vs. expiry date

These rules answer different questions. A limit concerns how many times a customer can download; expiry concerns how long the link remains available. Using both can make access more predictable, but the right settings depend on how customers use your files.

These figures are examples, not recommended defaults. Before using them, check the available settings in your app and make sure they match the terms you show customers.

How to choose rules customers can live with

Start with the customer’s real workflow. A buyer may download on a phone first, move to a laptop later, or need another attempt after a connection fails. A very low limit may create more support work than it saves.

Consider the type of product

A PDF guide might be saved immediately and read offline. A template pack may be opened on a second device or downloaded again when the buyer starts a new project. For large ZIP files, allow room for interrupted downloads. Consider a longer access period when customers are likely to return to their purchase later.

Make the terms clear

State the access period, download limit and contact method on the product page or in your purchase terms. For example: “Download your files up to three times within 14 days. Need help? Contact us.” Only use that wording if it accurately reflects your configured rules.

Plan for exceptions

Decide how you will handle a broken device, a missed email or a customer who reaches their limit by mistake. A clear support policy gives your team a consistent way to help without promising unrestricted access.

A practical setup checklist

  1. Connect the right files. Check that each product or variant is associated with the file the buyer expects.
  2. Choose your rules. On an eligible plan, set a download limit and expiry that fit your product and customer promise.
  3. Review the buyer journey. Make sure the product description and delivery message explain how to access the files and where to get help.
  4. Test a paid order. Follow the delivery flow as a customer, download the file, and verify that the access information behaves as expected.
  5. Prepare for support. Know where to review the order and download activity, resend a delivery email, and manage access if a customer needs help.

Settings and eligibility may vary by plan. Check the app’s current controls and plan details before publishing your store policy. If a feature or workflow is unclear, contact support before making it a customer promise.

What to do when a customer cannot download

First, confirm the order is paid and that the buyer is checking the correct email address. Then review the order and download activity: has the link expired, or has the available download count been used? Resending the delivery email does not by itself extend expired access, restore revoked access or reset the download count. With the appropriate access tools, you can resend the delivery email or manage access after checking the situation.

Tell the customer what you changed and what they should try next. A helpful reply might say: “I’ve checked your order and resent the delivery email. Please look in your inbox and spam folder, then contact us if the link still doesn’t work.” Adjust the message to reflect the action you actually took.

Frequently asked questions

Does Wix already deliver digital downloads?

Yes. Wix Stores sends a download link after payment and documents a 30-day period for its standard emailed link. Starli offers additional tools for merchants who want to manage files and access from an app.

Will download limits stop people from sharing my files?

No. Limits control the allowed downloads; they cannot prevent someone from copying or sharing a file after saving it. Explain permitted use separately in your license or terms.

What happens if a legitimate customer needs another download?

Review the order and the customer’s situation first. Depending on your plan and the controls available, you can resend the delivery email or manage their access. Set a support policy that is fair and easy to explain.

Do all Starli plans include custom download rules?

Feature availability depends on the plan. See the current Wix App Market listing for the latest plan details before choosing or changing your settings.

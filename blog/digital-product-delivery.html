WIX NOTES / DIGITAL PRODUCTS

How to deliver digital products in your Wix store without manual work

Selling a PDF, template or audio file should work even when you are away from your computer. But what happens after checkout? If every order means finding a file and sending an email yourself, delivery becomes another task on your list and your customer has to wait.

Digital Downloads lets you set up that journey in advance: connect files to products in your Wix store and deliver them automatically after payment.

From payment to download

Upload your files and connect them to a Wix product or variant. Once a paid order is ready for delivery, the customer automatically receives a private download link. They can open a page with their purchased files, without you having to send each email by hand.

This can be useful for PDF guides, design templates, audio files or ZIP bundles, for example. When a product has variants, you can connect files to a particular variant so the buyer receives the files associated with their choice.

Keep access manageable

Delivery does not end when a link is sent. You may want to set how long or how often a file can be downloaded, and customers sometimes need help. Set download limits and expiry dates in the app, then extend, restore or revoke access when needed.

You can see order activity, download counts and delivery errors in one place. That makes it easier to spot cases that need attention and resend a delivery email.

Make the next step clear for customers

A good product deserves a clear delivery experience. Customize the email message so customers know what they have received and how to get started. Their download page shows the files and any download limits or expiry dates that apply.

Before you start selling, walk through the entire journey yourself: check the product description, make a test purchase, open the email on your phone and download the file. Confirm each product variant is connected to the right files and that customers know where to ask for help.

When is it worth trying?

If you already sell digital files on Wix or are preparing your first product, Digital Downloads brings delivery and access management together. Set up a clear process in advance so you can spend more of your time on the product itself.

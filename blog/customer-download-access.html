How to help a customer who cannot access their download

Written November 3, 2026 · Starli Labs

Illustrative app view.

A customer has paid, but their download is nowhere to be found—or the link no longer works. A calm, consistent support process helps you identify what happened and choose the right next step. Start with the order and access status; sending the same email again is not always the fix.

Starli Digital Downloads brings order, delivery and download activity together, with tools to resend delivery emails and manage access. Availability of access-management features depends on your plan; check the current plan details.

Start with the order, not the link

Ask the customer for their order number and the email address used at checkout. Look up the order and check whether payment has been received, which product or variant was purchased, and which files are associated with it. If the payment is still pending, follow your store’s process for confirming it before promising delivery.

Be careful not to ask a customer to post their private download link publicly. If someone contacts you from a different email address, verify the order through your normal support process before changing the delivery recipient or sharing access.

Find out which part of delivery failed

“I can’t download” can describe several different situations. Ask what the customer sees: did the email fail to arrive, did the link open an error, or did the file download but not open? A screenshot of an error message can help, but ask the customer to hide personal information and avoid including the full private link.

  • No delivery email: Check the order’s delivery activity and the recipient address. Ask the customer to check spam and any filtered inboxes.
  • Link opens but access is unavailable: Review whether the order’s access has expired, reached its download limit, or been stopped.
  • The wrong file appears: Compare the purchased product and variant with the file mapping and the order’s details.
  • The file downloaded but won’t open: Check the file format, file size and whether the customer has an appropriate app or enough device storage.

Activity records give you useful clues, but treat them as a starting point. A customer may have tried another browser or had an interrupted download; ask before assuming what happened.

Resend an email when the customer needs the link again

If access is still available and the customer simply cannot find the delivery email, resending it may be enough. First verify the recipient, then use the delivery-email action available for the order. Let the customer know which address to check and remind them to look in spam or filtered folders.

Resending an email does not itself extend an expired link, restore stopped access, or reset a used download count. If one of those conditions is preventing access, review and address it separately before asking the customer to try again. A new message alone may otherwise lead them back to the same problem.

Review the rules when access has ended

A customer can run into a limit for an understandable reason: they changed devices, the first download was interrupted, or they returned after the access period. Compare the order’s status and current rules with the terms shown when the product was sold.

Depending on your plan and the order’s situation, Starli provides controls to change download limits or expiry, reset the download count, and stop or restore access. Choose the action that matches the problem and your support policy. Restoring stopped access does not necessarily resolve a separate expiry or limit, so check the complete status before closing the request.

For example, if a customer missed the access window while travelling, you might decide to extend access. If they exhausted their allowed downloads during an interrupted connection, review the count and decide whether a reset is appropriate. Tell them what you actually changed and when they can try again.

Handle a wrong file or a file that won’t open

For a wrong download, verify the exact variant on the order and compare it with your product mapping. Correct the mapping for future sales as needed, then review the particular order and choose a suitable way to help that customer. Do not assume changing a product’s files automatically replaces the files in an existing order; check the order and your version workflow first.

If the download completes but the file cannot be opened, try the same file yourself on a representative device. A ZIP archive needs to be extracted, and some formats need specific software. A short instruction in the delivery email or product description can prevent confusion, especially for less familiar formats.

A useful reply template

Keep the response specific to the action taken:

Hi [name], thanks for getting in touch. I’ve checked order [number] and [describe the action taken]. Please check [where to find the email or file] and try again. If you still see an error, let me know what it says and which device you’re using.

Before sending, replace the brackets and make sure every statement is true for that order. Avoid promising unlimited access unless that is genuinely your policy.

Frequently asked questions

Should I resend the link or extend access?

Resend when the customer needs the email and access remains valid. If the link has expired or the limit has been reached, review the access rules first. These are separate actions.

Can I restore a customer’s downloads?

Starli offers controls to stop and restore access on eligible plans. Check expiry and download limits as well: a restored status does not automatically remove every other restriction.

Will changing a product file update a previous purchase?

Do not assume it will. Check the order and your file-version workflow before making a promise about an existing purchase.

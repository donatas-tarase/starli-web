Digital Downloads PDF+ overview

  • Set download limits and expiry
  • Deliver the right files for each product or variant
  • Send download links automatically after paid orders
  • Manage customer access

A sale is just the beginning. Digital Downloads delivers your files after payment and gives you control over what happens next.

Set download limits and expiry rules, connect the right files to products or variants, and see download activity in one dashboard.

A customer needs help? Check their order, resend the delivery email, or manage their access.

Sell PDFs, ebooks, templates, presets, audio files and ZIP bundles with a delivery experience you can manage.

Custom access rules and access management are included in Pro and Enterprise. Custom email design is available on Enterprise. Check plan details for limits and availability.

Search & Filter for Wix

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes data handling in Product Search & Filter (the “App”), provided by Starli Labs, MB. It supplements our company Privacy Policy, which describes our contact details, general processing, international transfers, privacy rights and policy updates. Digital Downloads has its own policy.

Your saved setup: uninstalling stops App search and removes rebuildable catalog and raw visitor data during cleanup. Search settings and rules remain available for a same-site reinstall. You can clear saved configuration in Settings or request full data erasure through our privacy contact.

1. Information the App handles

  • Installation and merchant data: Wix site and app-instance identifiers, locale, subscription metadata, search settings, appearance, filters, synonyms, merchandising rules, product inclusion choices and setup status.
  • Product catalog: product and variant identifiers, names, descriptions, categories, options, SKU, images, product links, prices, currency, visibility and availability needed to provide product search.
  • Search activity: queries, generated session and search identifiers, result counts, product interactions, filter and sort selections, timestamps and bounded runtime context, when the site's analytics consent permits collection.
  • Technical and support information: sync and error status, request correlation identifiers, setup verification records, and messages or merchant contact details used for support. Our dashboard support tool may receive the site, instance, plan and merchant contact information associated with a support session.

The App does not collect payment-card numbers. Product search does not require a shopper account. Search phrases can contain personal information; avoid entering unnecessary personal data in queries, synonyms or rules.

2. Purposes and service providers

We receive catalog and installation information from Wix and configuration from the merchant. We use it to operate product search, apply the merchant's settings, synchronize the catalog, diagnose failures, verify setup and provide support. We use consent-dependent search analytics to report search usage and results to the merchant.

Wix provides the site platform and app hosting. Supabase provides our database and background processing; Algolia provides the current search index. Our own database may also answer searches when the index is unavailable or an exact catalog operation requires it. Crisp provides dashboard support chat. Relevant information is processed by these services to deliver those functions. Provider logs and infrastructure data may have separate retention from the App's live data stores.

General roles, processing grounds, service-provider sharing and international transfers are described in our company Privacy Policy. If you are a shopper, contact the merchant first about a request concerning its store; we assist with App-held data as applicable.

3. Analytics consent and setup checks

Search works when analytics consent is refused. Behavioral analytics use the site's Wix consent policy, generated identifiers and event deduplication. Dashboard tests, editor previews, Wix preview and explicitly marked merchant checks are separated from ordinary live-usage metrics. Unmarked live sessions can also include merchant testing; they are not proof of an independent shopper.

An intentional published-site check uses a tenant-bound token valid for 30 minutes, a search identifier, bounded returned product identifiers and technical observations of search and product opening. It records setup status separately from behavioral analytics and does not require analytics consent. Owner-confirmed checks are identified as owner confirmation rather than system verification.

4. Retention and uninstall

  • While installed: catalog and configuration are maintained to operate the App. Raw behavioral analytics have a 90-day retention period; daily search aggregates have a 25-month expiry.
  • On uninstall: serving is disabled and pending external writes are fenced. Durable cleanup removes the rebuildable catalog and search index, raw behavioral events, visitor search sessions, verification tokens, webhook and processing payloads and tenant processing queues. Cleanup can retry when a provider is unavailable.
  • For reinstall: the installation identity, saved settings, filters, synonyms, rules and inclusion choices remain under the same authenticated Wix app instance, so that a same-site reinstall can restore configuration. The catalog is rebuilt. Previous setup checks may need to be repeated.
  • Aggregates: existing daily aggregates keep their 25-month expiry after uninstall. They are separate from the removed raw visitor events.
  • Deletion: verified full data-erasure requests take precedence over ordinary App retention. Infrastructure backups, security logs, support correspondence and legally required records are handled separately as described in the company policy.

Retaining App configuration does not restore Wix page layout or native search components. Uninstalling the App does not itself cancel a Wix subscription.

5. Your controls and deletion requests

You can pause App search under Website Search → Setup. Pausing leaves saved configuration in place and does not publish or remove Wix elements. The “Return to Wix search” instructions describe the owner actions needed in the editor.

“Delete saved configuration” in Settings clears merchant search overrides, filters, synonyms, rules, inclusion choices and current setup checks. Installation and milestone history remain until full App data erasure. This action leaves analytics and Wix layout unchanged. For access or full App data erasure, including after uninstall, contact privacy@starli.app and identify your Wix site. We verify that you are authorized before acting. Do not email passwords or app secrets.

For product support, contact searchandfilter@starli.app. Your privacy rights and the process for contacting us are also described in our company Privacy Policy.

6. Updates and contact

We publish changes here with an updated date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or another appropriate channel, as described in our company policy.

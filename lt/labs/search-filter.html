Product Search & Filter apžvalga

  • Instant product search with live suggestions and a dedicated results page
  • Custom search design and responsive filters for desktop and mobile
  • Synonyms, redirects and pinned products for smarter merchandising
  • Automatic catalog sync with search, click, no-result and revenue insights

Search & Filter helps online store shoppers find the right products with fast search and intuitive filters.

Keep the search experience aligned with your catalog through automated product syncing. Let shoppers narrow results by category, price, availability, and product options.

From one dashboard, you can configure search behavior, manage filters, synonyms, and merchandising rules, monitor catalog sync health, preview results, and review search analytics.

Add the search bar to your site, customize their placement in the site editor, and give shoppers a clearer path from query to product.

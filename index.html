THE BEGINNING / CLOSE TO PEOPLE

I came to products
through people.

I wanted two things: to work at a computer and to talk to people. After my first jobs in construction, I found my way into customer support - a call centre and other customer support teams. The systems and questions changed; the desire to understand them stayed.

I enjoyed quickly seeing how things fit together and explaining them to someone else. Sometimes I was still new myself when I started helping another newcomer. That is where I learned to first find out what someone wanted to do and where they got stuck.

My family gave me the habit of helping, and I probably inherited a good deal of perfectionism from my grandmother. I still find something to fix when everyone else thinks it is perfect. Useful when building apps. Perhaps not always so convenient when living with me.

NEXT / FROM ANSWERING TO BUILDING

I came to answer.
I started building.

My customer support experience led to an opportunity through LinkedIn to work on an ecommerce product. I visited the office in Vilnius, took in a huge amount of information in one day and already knew I wanted to work there.

I started with users’ questions. Testing followed, then programming and more responsibility for maintaining the product. Today my work covers the journey from implementing and checking a solution to user requests and payments.

That growth happened through everyday work: a task comes up, you need to figure it out, you do, and next time you know a little more. When someone takes the time to tell you your work helped, you want to keep building.

MY FIRST OWN APP / STARLI MIND

First, I built
something for myself.

Starli Mind began with a personal need. I wanted a place for conversation and reflection about relationships that would retain the context of my situation.

I worked out the visuals, features and logic, tried it on my phone and gradually built the product I wanted to have. I liked using it myself, so I decided to release it for others.

That iOS app was the beginning of building independently. It reminds me of a feeling I still enjoy: thinking of something I’m missing and being able to start making it.

A NEW STEP / STARLI LABS

My own ideas
needed a home.

Later, I began looking at what Wix users were missing. I already knew them from working with Wix apps: how they expected settings to work, what made sense to them and where questions usually arose.

I wanted the products to grow under a company name and to turn this work into a business of my own. That is how Starli Labs came about in 2026. I do a lot myself at this stage; one day I would like to grow a team, too.

DIGITAL DOWNLOADS / FIRST REVENUE

The first subscription
called for cake.

Digital Downloads became my first own app to earn revenue. When someone bought an annual subscription, it was hard to believe at first: people were not just using something I had built, but paying for it.

My wife bought cake and champagne. I remember that evening better than the exact subscription amount. It was a very tangible encouragement to continue. Search & Filter and other ideas for Wix users followed.

AND ALSO / MATHEMATICS

My teacher
already knew.

When I started thinking about returning to university, I remembered something my mathematics teacher had said:

Donatas, you’ll see - when you grow up, you will definitely be a teacher.

That sentence had stayed with me. I have always enjoyed understanding something and helping someone else understand it too - I noticed that long before, while working in customer support.

Now I’m studying mathematics pedagogy at Vytautas Magnus University and want to teach mathematics in the future. Alongside that, I’m continuing to grow my ecommerce and SaaS work. There is room for both in my life.
